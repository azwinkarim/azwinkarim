## Five markets. The problem, my part, the impact.

Each case is three lines: what was in the way, what I did, and what changed.

01 Off-Highway

### What if the opportunity was bigger than the market we knew?

Problem

Off-Highway marketing focused on FMCE vehicles. Other specialised vehicle makers weren’t on the list.

My contribution

Researched adjacent segments (material handling, ATVs, golf carts), recommended where our engineering could translate, built the collateral and opened first conversations with the Engineering Industry Head.

Impact

New customer logos and account wins that grew the business. Sales closed them.

02 Japan automotive

### When the market doesn’t speak your language, build a better way to listen.

Problem

Little usable market information online, plus language and relationship barriers to new customers.

My contribution

Built access through trade bodies, research agencies and overseas Japanese offices. Localised messaging with the Japan team. Ran the Japan Automotive Roadshow, the first in five years.

Impact

Roadshow: 14 qualified leads, 4 active conversations. Supported about 30% Japan business growth (FY25 Q3 to FY26 Q4), alongside wider team efforts.

03 Aerospace

### Don’t just target an industry. Find the teams that need you.

Problem

Relationships sat with Tier-1 suppliers. A path to OEM engineering teams didn’t exist yet.

My contribution

Mapped which OEM departments buy engineering services. Built targeted outreach, thought leadership and events, then qualified leads with sales before handover.

Impact

Meetings with three leading global OEMs and early engagement with eVTOL companies. Not every meeting converted.

04 Rail

### Sometimes the smartest move is not the biggest event.

Problem

After team attrition, Rail needed momentum on a small budget.

My contribution

Picked a few events, a seminar and booth activity built around specific accounts rather than footfall.

Impact

Two key Rail engagements initiated and still in progress. Not yet closed wins.

05 Global new-customer marketing

### Make new-customer growth a repeatable way of working.

Problem

A global remit needed one rhythm across verticals and regions, not separate campaigns.

My contribution

Lead new-customer marketing across Automotive, Rail and Off-Highway in EMEA, the Americas, APAC and Japan. Align account priorities, programmes and sales teams.

Impact

$3.5M FY27 pipeline under the expanded remit.

The $10M won business TCV (FY24 $3M, FY25 $1M, FY26 $3.5M, FY27 $2.5M so far) covers opportunities that marketing generated or substantially supported. It does not imply sole ownership of sales closure. FY27 pipeline is a separate, forward-looking measure.
